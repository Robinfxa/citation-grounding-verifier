## Purpose

Measure factual verification, evidence integrity and claim coverage through reproducible comparisons that preserve difficult cases and operational failures in the scoring population.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Balanced frozen benchmark
The benchmark SHALL contain at least 32 original labeled cases with balanced coverage of supported answers, subtle distortions, extrinsic unsupported assertions and multi-passage synthesis, plus explicit edge cases. Gold annotations SHALL contain atomic claim text, verdict, brief rationale, acceptable answer-text aliases when needed and exact minimal sufficient evidence alternatives. Development and test partitions SHALL be explicit, disjoint by family and fixed before tuning; annotation provenance and limitations SHALL be disclosed.

#### Scenario: Dataset validation
- **WHEN** a case has an invalid citation, unsupported claim with evidence, duplicate identifier or family crossing the dev/test partition
- **THEN** dataset validation fails before the benchmark starts

### Requirement: Three comparable implementations
The runner SHALL compare a deterministic non-LLM heuristic, a minimal single-call structured LLM approach and an optimized decomposition/evidence-verification approach. LLM variants SHALL use the same explicitly configured model, provider, dataset and operational limits. Tests with scripted responses SHALL be labeled offline tests and MUST NOT be reported as real LLM quality measurements.

#### Scenario: Missing model credentials
- **WHEN** the benchmark is run without model configuration
- **THEN** the heuristic can be measured and LLM results are explicitly unavailable rather than generated by a hidden mock or heuristic fallback

### Requirement: Separate verification from extraction
The runner SHALL offer an oracle-claim track that supplies the same gold claim texts to each implementation, and an end-to-end track that supplies the original answer. Oracle outputs SHALL preserve input claim identity and cardinality. End-to-end scoring SHALL use verdict-blind one-to-one matching and penalize omitted and extra claims. Strict normalized text and preregistered aliases SHALL be the auditable headline alignment; fuzzy suggestions SHALL NOT silently count as correct matches. Correct but unrecognized paraphrases SHALL be disclosed as a limitation and available for review.

#### Scenario: Omission and duplication
- **WHEN** a verifier returns one correct claim while omitting three gold claims, or duplicates one claim
- **THEN** its coverage and end-to-end correctness are reduced; the denominator is not the number of returned claims alone

### Requirement: Transparent metrics and uncertainty
Reports SHALL include claim-level per-label precision/recall/F1 and macro F1, end-to-end matched-claim coverage and joint label correctness, exact case success, literal citation validity, and gold evidence-set coverage as a distinct diagnostic. Evidence-set coverage SHALL NOT be mislabeled as independently established semantic entailment. Reports SHALL retain errors in requested-case denominators and show failure categories. Aggregated confidence intervals SHALL resample cases with fixed seeds and state the small hand-authored benchmark's limited generalizability.

#### Scenario: One evaluation call fails
- **WHEN** one requested case fails after retries
- **THEN** the run records its operational error and preserves its gold claims in relevant recall/success denominators rather than skipping the case

### Requirement: Reproducible resource and iteration records
The runner SHALL save configuration, dataset and prompt/code fingerprints, per-case results, wall-clock latency and provider usage when available. Costs SHALL be reported only from explicitly recorded prices and measured token usage; unavailable usage or prices SHALL remain unknown. Existing run artifacts SHALL not be overwritten accidentally. Development failure analysis and prompt changes SHALL be traceable; unmeasured expected improvements MUST NOT be presented as observed hill climbing.

#### Scenario: Live measurement blocked
- **WHEN** model credentials are absent or a live run is incomplete
- **THEN** the README distinguishes implemented methods, offline checks and actual live measurements, with commands to complete the remaining comparison
