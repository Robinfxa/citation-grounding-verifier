These two artificial English cases calibrate measurement, independently of both benchmark versions. They are never model-quality evidence.

- `calibration-logic`: four gold claims (one S, two C, one U). A first-match C→S and U→S produce false support 2/3 even if the remaining C is omitted. Operational failure keeps 3/3 non-S gold in the denominator with 0 judgment coverage. The rate alone cannot imply safety when judgments are missing.
- An unregistered paraphrase, changed number, negation, scope, punctuation, case or Unicode normalization cannot match by similarity. A frozen alias and whitespace-only changes can match. A merged prediction never earns two matches. A second prediction for an already matched gold remains an extra; its more favorable verdict cannot replace the first prediction.
- `calibration-relations`: both claims need an entity link from passage 0 and retention from passage 1. The actual returned quote must cover every member of a gold evidence group. Citing an index alone, a nearby entity or only a partial quote is insufficient. A full-passage quotation can satisfy span coverage but spends more quote characters; neither establishes semantic sufficiency.
- Repeated measurements of these two cases remain two source families. `(case_id, repetition)` identifies an observation. A full run plus two subset runs yields three observations only for the chosen subset. Missing or duplicate observations and incompatible configurations are rejected.
