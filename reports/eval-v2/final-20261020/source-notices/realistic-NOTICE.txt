This directory includes historical AI-authored drafts and the reviewed
main-candidate. The candidate metadata binds the accepted independent AI
annotation reviews; it is awaiting parent promotion and is not human-reviewed.

Contexts are unchanged Unicode slices of the supplied upstream UTF-8 files.
The manifest records the exact source revision, raw hash, slice coordinates,
passage hash and full retained permission notice. Markup and code examples
remain unchanged. Questions and answers were newly authored by an AI coding
session; they are not recorded production-user questions or model responses
borrowed from another benchmark. Controlled changes, when present, are
identified separately and retain their base answer.
