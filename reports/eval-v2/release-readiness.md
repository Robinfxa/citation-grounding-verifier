### Two corrections and one uncertain alignment

The [original sidecar](final-20261020/semantic-audit-adjudication.json) includes exact quotes, original votes, locks and reviewer acknowledgements. This review read the corresponding original case, prediction and source, not just the explanation:

- **r34-c07 / O prediction 7, batch01 item-0000**: the answer says rewriting can still work without cached bytecode; gold connects that to assertion introspection. The returned p3 quote explicitly says rewriting silently skips caching when it cannot write `.pyc` files. Under the locked equivalent interpretation, demanding another explicit “still get benefits” sentence was excessive. Original `insufficient` is preserved; the sensitivity uses `sufficient`. Category **E**, with the existing alignment premise retained.
- **r20-c10 / O prediction 5, batch02 item-0007**: the claim already presupposes a dispatchable method and describes lifetime permission in a narrower no-Self setting. The p1 quote explicitly permits lifetime parameters on dispatchable functions. A general permission supports this narrower case; it does not assert that the no-Self condition alone makes a method dispatchable. Category **E**. Original insufficient vote remains; the sensitivity changes only evidence.
- **r04-c09 / O prediction 1, batch02 item-0017**: answer anchor “passing InitVar arguments in declaration order”; gold says **when** arguments are passed, their order is declaration order (S). Prediction asserts unconditional forwarding in declaration order, labels U and supplies no quote. Source p0 qualifies calling the hook by “When defined on the class”. Stage 1 locked equivalent; stage 2 explicitly records an uncertain label and alignment erratum. **B/E unresolved**; no automatic label/evidence credit. Human review should decide scope equivalence first, not force equal labels on different propositions.

The two evidence changes yield O **200/546 → 204/546**, or .366300 → .373626; gold/prediction denominators, original votes, alignment and labels do not change. M is unchanged. Acknowledgements are recorded as parent-relayed original-reviewer confirmations, not separately signed reviewer files or new human decisions.
